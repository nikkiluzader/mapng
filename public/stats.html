    Take the numbers

    Everything on this page is drawn in your browser from four public files. Nothing else feeds it.

    • /api/hotspots: every shared export square, exactly as stored
    • /api/usage: every settings total, exactly as stored
    • /api/stats: visits, problem report counts and downloads
    • /country_rules.json: country outlines (Natural Earth), used to name the country a square is in

    What you will never find here

    • A name, account, email or device ID: MapNG has none
    • An internet address
    • An exact location: a square is about 5 km across
    • A level's name or contents
    • Anything from inside a problem report
    • A country with fewer than 25 visits
    • Anything from someone who didn't opt in to sharing exports or settings

    The code that makes these numbers: worker/publicStats.js and worker/hotspots.js. The full account of what MapNG collects: Your data and MapNG.
