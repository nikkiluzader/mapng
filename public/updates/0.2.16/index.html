Smoother junctions on hills

A small update for a bug a tester found within hours of 0.2.15: ridges and flat pads across the road wherever two roads meet on a slope.

Roads

🛣️ No more ridges across the road at junctions

On a hill, a junction could come out with a flat pad across the main road, a short steep ramp beside it, and the side road's trench cut into the main road's near lane. It happened at every terrain resolution; on a map made at 0.25 m per pixel it showed as a sharp ridge.

There were five causes, and all five are fixed:

  • Road smoothing averaged a road's height by counting its points, not by distance. Points are closer together near corners, so the road stepped beside every junction on a slope.
  • A wide road's levelled strip reached past the junction at the wrong height and left a pad across it (0.5 m off on the tester's 12 % hill).
  • A road steeper than its class allows was held to its limit along most of the hill and then let go at three times that for the last metres before the junction. It now follows the hill at one even grade.
  • A main road changing grade at a junction did it in a corner, because its two halves were worked out as two roads. It now gets a rounded curve through the junction, about 3 m of road per percent of change.
  • A side road started dropping from the main road's centre line. It now stays on the main road's surface across its lanes and leaves the edge at the main road's slope.
Good to know: a road down a hill that is steeper than its class would be built now follows the hill instead of cutting deep into it. On very steep terrain that means a steep road, as the elevation data has it.
Map data

🗺️ MapNG tells you when it used backup map data

When OpenStreetMap's servers do not answer, MapNG builds from Overture Maps, a monthly copy. That copy has no lane counts (a four-lane road can come out as two lanes) and marks bridges only roughly (some come out short or missing). Until now only a small chip said so.

After a Generate that used the backup, a notice over the map now says what is missing and suggests generating again later. Two more changes make the backup less likely in the first place: one failed request no longer makes MapNG give up on its own map data tiles, and the export report now says why they were not used.
